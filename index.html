🌸 About Diwali

Festival of Happiness and Light

Diwali is one of the most popular festivals celebrated in India. It is also known as the Festival of Lights.

People celebrate Diwali by lighting diyas, decorating their homes, creating rangoli, exchanging sweets and spending time with family and friends.

The festival represents the victory of good over evil and light over darkness.

⚠️ Diwali Safety Tips

🔥 Fire Safety

Keep diyas away from curtains and flammable objects.

🎆 Fireworks

Avoid fireworks and never allow children to handle them.

🌱 Environment

Prefer eco-friendly decorations and celebrate responsibly.

🩹 First Aid
